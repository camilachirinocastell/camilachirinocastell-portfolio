I studied frontend, backend, UX and generative AI because I want to understand the product as a whole.

Before development, I led teams, worked in sales, and built a career in design and illustration. Today I apply that same logic to code: understand the problem, think through the solution, execute it well — with technical judgment, visual sensibility, and the ability to communicate across teams.

I completed the Frontend and Backend Development careers at ADA ITW simultaneously, and today I'm building agentic AI systems with LangChain and LangGraph while serving as an AI Tutor at Samsung Innovation Campus 2026 — oriented toward the AI Engineer path. I also run Trece Art Studio, my own design, illustration and web development studio, with over 3 years of real client work, including Cementerio de Libros (1.7M Instagram followers).
